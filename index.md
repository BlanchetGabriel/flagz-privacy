# Flagz Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-12_

Flagz is a flag-quiz game. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## What the app stores
Your game progress (scores, streaks, settings, and which flags you have learned) is saved **only on your device**, using the app's local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or using **Settings → Reset all progress**, deletes it. If you have Android's device backup enabled, Android may include this local data in your own Google backup; that is handled by Google, not by us.

## What the app does not do
- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising
- No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files
- No network connection is needed to play; all flags and sounds are bundled with the app

## Sharing
If you tap **Share result**, the text of your score is handed to the Android share sheet and sent to whichever app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Children
Flagz does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
